NeriFlow

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 3, 2026

NeriFlow is a construction field operations platform provided by Paret Holdings LLC through Beyond Tide Solutions. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, stored, and protected when authorized users access NeriFlow.

1. Information We Collect

NeriFlow may collect information necessary to provide and operate the platform, including:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • User and account identifiers
  • Project and job-related information entered by authorized users
  • Daily reports, notes, forms, quantities, and other operational records
  • Photos or other files uploaded to projects, reports, or records

2. How We Use Information

Information collected through NeriFlow is used to provide and support the functionality of the platform. This may include:

  • Authenticating users and managing account access
  • Providing construction project and field operations functionality
  • Processing and storing project records and daily reports
  • Allowing authorized users to upload and access project attachments
  • Maintaining platform security and reliability
  • Providing technical and customer support

3. Photos and Uploaded Content

NeriFlow allows authorized users to capture or select photos and upload files for use with construction projects, reports, and related records. Camera and photo library access is used only when a user chooses to use these features.

Uploaded project content is used to provide NeriFlow's project and field operations functionality and is not used for advertising or tracking.

4. Tracking and Advertising

NeriFlow does not use collected personal information for third-party advertising or cross-app tracking. NeriFlow does not sell personal information to data brokers for advertising purposes.

5. Information Sharing

Information may be processed by service providers that support the operation, hosting, storage, security, and delivery of NeriFlow. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect the security, rights, or integrity of NeriFlow, its customers, or users.

6. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards designed to protect information maintained through NeriFlow. However, no electronic transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Data Retention

Information is retained as reasonably necessary to provide NeriFlow, maintain customer and project records, meet contractual obligations, comply with applicable legal requirements, and protect legitimate business interests.

8. Access and Data Requests

NeriFlow is primarily provided to organizations for use by their authorized personnel. Requests concerning access, correction, or deletion of information associated with an organization may be handled through that organization or by contacting Beyond Tide Solutions.

9. Children's Privacy

NeriFlow is a business application intended for authorized workforce users and is not directed to children.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as NeriFlow changes or as legal and operational requirements evolve. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

11. Contact Us

Questions or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or NeriFlow's privacy practices may be submitted through the Beyond Tide Solutions contact page.
